The first two planned events are Infinity Weekend and Daily Puzzle Week. October's additional event highlights custom themes; November highlights replay; December highlights Challenge mode. These are time-bounded editorial spotlights on existing features. Google requires promotional content to be specific and sufficiently distinct; do not submit repeated monthly copy or treat the schedule as automatic approval. If a store rejects a feature spotlight as routine use rather than a distinct event, omit it there instead of implying a new mode or benefit.

### Store eligibility and activation gates

- Apple In-App Events need a timely, specific in-app experience. The listed routes lead to existing, always-available features, so each Apple event is a conditional draft: submit only if App Review accepts the limited-duration editorial framing as a specific event experience. If not, keep an eligible Play entry only or remove the item from that store's calendar. Do not describe Infinity as a competition, or Daily Puzzle Week as a tracked streak contest.
- The app has a daily puzzle and displays an existing play streak, but does not have an event-specific streak counter, reward, leaderboard, or special event state. Copy only promises the existing daily puzzle, history replay, themes, difficulty, or solo Challenge mode.
- The event artwork kit tracked in [issue #473](https://github.com/vitalyiegorov/suuudokuuu/issues/473) is pending. Artwork is an activation dependency for store submission, not for this planning calendar. Do not submit until the required platform artwork has been created, reviewed, localized as needed, and checked against current asset specs.
- Play promotional content availability is account- and title-eligibility dependent. Confirm Promotional Content is available in the app's Play Console before committing to this cadence.

## Policy and route references

Rules checked 2026-10-08 against the current vendor guidance:

- [Apple In-App Events](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events/) gives event name, short description, and long description limits of 30, 50, and 120 characters. [App Store Connect event guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-in-app-events/offer-in-app-events/) allows at most 10 published events at once, event durations up to 31 days, and a discoverability date up to 14 days before start. The 14-day value is the maximum advance promotion window, not a mandatory submission lead time. This schedule uses that window where possible.
- [Google Play promotional content](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12932541) sets an 80-character tagline and 500-character description maximum. Its current time-limited event rules cap duration at four weeks. Submission opens no earlier than 60 days before the start; approval may take up to 4 days. A featuring request must be submitted at least 14 days ahead and only applies when the event meets Google's qualifying conditions. The schedule requests featuring only where that lead time remains; requests are not guarantees of selection.
- App route evidence: `src/app/(tabs)/daily.tsx` renders `DailyScreen`; `src/screens/components/home-screen/home.screen.tsx` provides difficulty selection and Challenge mode; `src/app/settings/themes/index.tsx` is the theme route; `src/app/history/[difficulty]/index.tsx` lists completed games and `src/app/history/[difficulty]/[completedAt].tsx` opens replay. No event-specific URL parameters are assumed. On routes that need a user choice, the copy names the next action.
