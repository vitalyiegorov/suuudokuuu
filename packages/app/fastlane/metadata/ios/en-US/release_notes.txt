Import any puzzle, with clearer hints.

• Paste a puzzle (digits, grid text, or a link from other sudoku sites) or open an import link. Check it on a board you can tap to correct, see that it has exactly one solution and its difficulty, then play.
• Hints for pairs and triples are explained in plain, complete sentences, show the candidates involved, and fit small screens and large text sizes.
• Fixes: completed grids are rejected on import, large or unusual pastes are read correctly, the number pad resizes properly, settings such as language are saved reliably, keyboard focus stays inside sheets on the web, and the app opens in a second browser tab.
