Smarter hints, a bigger board.

• Hints now teach step by step, with Back, Next and Show answer, and a spinner while a hint is found. The hardest puzzles get hints too.
• New techniques: Empty Rectangle, Sue de Coq and ALS-XZ are spotted and explained in hints.
• The board is bigger on phones, and a technique pill shows your latest technique in every run.
• Back up your games, statistics and themes to a file, and import them on another device.
• The timer follows the wall clock so scores stay fair, and the result screen opens right away.
• Moves placed with a hint are badged in the replay, and the iOS edge blur is back.
