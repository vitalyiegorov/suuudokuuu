Hints now teach instead of telling. Each one names the technique, lights up the cells that force the move, and walks you through the reasoning step by step before anything is placed.

• Undo and redo, with honest scoring: an undo hands back exactly the points its placement earned, so replaying the same cell can never pay twice.
• Hints stay off Nightmare, Hell and Infinity unless you switch them on, and both hints and undo are hidden in Hardcore.
• Daily challenge on its own tab: the same puzzle for everyone each day, with a current and best streak and your recent solves.
• Difficulty now means what it says — every board is generated until it really needs the techniques its level promises.
• Kinder to hands and eyes: bigger touch targets on the board and number pad, a reduce motion setting, larger digits, Calm play without a score, and full respect for your device text size.
• New High contrast and Colorblind safe themes, with every board color pair checked against WCAG AA and states told apart by lightness and outlines instead of hue.
• Full screen reader support: cells, digits and controls announce their role and state, and hint steps, mistakes and wins are spoken as they happen.
• The Notes control now sits alone at the thumb edge, mirrored for left-handed play, and on desktop the hint panel finally has a readable size.
• Fixed: moves built from several logical steps are credited to the technique behind them instead of counting as guesses, auto-candidates now drop what a hint eliminated, and the white digit on the selected cell is back.
• Faster: the web version paints straight away instead of waiting for the whole download, and the app itself is 5.5 MB smaller.
