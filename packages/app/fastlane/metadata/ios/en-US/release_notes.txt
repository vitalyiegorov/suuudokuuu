Hints that teach step by step, faster new games and smoother keyboard play.

• Hints now open in four steps: first the technique and where to look, then the key cells, then a guided walkthrough, and only then the move itself. Each step costs a quarter of a full hint, so you only pay for the help you use.
• Hints head straight for the next digit you can place. Pointing, box-line and subset hints now name the exact box, row or column and the cells they clear.
• Continue and Reveal work on every slide of a hint.
• New puzzles are generated in the background, so starting even the hardest game no longer freezes the app.
• Playing with a hardware keyboard on iPhone and iPad no longer keeps the processor busy.
• Links from the App Store and our website open the app with a matching difficulty ready to start.
