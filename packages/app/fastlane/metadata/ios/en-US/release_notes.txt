Hints that teach, a technique campaign and richer stats.

• Hints are now a compact step-by-step panel with Back, Next and a clear Show answer. The board stays full size, and your open hint survives a pause.
• New guided technique campaign with technique-mastery stats, so you can see which skills you have truly learned.
• Moves placed with a hint are badged in the replay, and history cards show how many hints you used.
• Hints are now on for the hardest puzzles too.
• Hardware keyboard input is more reliable on iPhone and iPad.
• Chain and branch links on the board are easier to read.
