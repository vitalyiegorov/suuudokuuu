suuudokuuu is Sudoku the way it should be: fast, beautiful, and honest about difficulty. Six levels, from a gentle Newbie board to genuine Hell — the hardest legitimate Sudoku that exists. No ads pretending to be puzzles, no accounts, no nonsense. Just you and the grid.

NEW LOOK AND FEEL
We rebuilt the board from the color up. Pick Classic, Gold, or Newspaper, or open the live-preview theme editor and make every cell, line, and highlight exactly yours. Built-in contrast validation keeps every custom theme readable in both light and dark, so your board always looks sharp and never strains your eyes.

HELL DIFFICULTY, FOR REAL
Most "expert" Sudoku apps fake their hardest level. Ours doesn't. Hell difficulty draws from a bundled, verified corpus of 49,158 genuine 17-clue puzzles — the minimum number of clues a Sudoku can have and still be uniquely solvable. If you've ever finished an "expert" puzzle and felt shortchanged, this is the level that will finally push back.

CHALLENGE A FRIEND
Rival mode sends your exact puzzle to a friend as a share link, with your recorded run attached. They play the same grid you did and race your time move for move. Bragging rights, settled.

WATCH IT BACK
Every completed game can be replayed, move by move. Rewind your own solve, study where you slowed down, or just enjoy watching a good finish twice.

SMARTER SCORING
Our scoring isn't just a timer. It's technique-aware, recognizing real solving moves like Naked Pairs and X-Wing as you play, so your score reflects how you actually solved the puzzle, not just how fast you clicked.
