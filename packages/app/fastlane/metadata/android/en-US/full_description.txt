suuudokuuu is a Sudoku puzzle game with seven difficulty levels, from Newbie to Infinity, and a Sudoku Explainer (SE) rating on every board. Play classic sudoku offline with no account, take on a daily challenge, learn techniques from step-by-step hints, and send a friend your exact puzzle. No ads.

EVERY PUZZLE, RATED
Every puzzle carries a Sudoku Explainer rating on the 1.0 to 12.0 scale serious solvers use, shown right in the level pill on the board, in your history, and on every link you share. A built-in explainer tells you what the number actually measures — how hard the puzzle is to reason through, not how few clues it has.

INFINITY: PUZZLES RATED SE 10.4 TO 11.9
A curated set of 100 puzzles rated SE 10.4 to 11.9, including Arto Inkala's Everest and AI Escargot.

HELL: FORCING CHAINS REQUIRED
Hell is a curated set of verified puzzles that all need a forcing chain: none of them can be finished with the techniques Nightmare allows, and every one is rated SE 7.5 or higher.

YOUR SOLVING, MEASURED
The statistics screen shows your hardest solve, your recent average difficulty and the level you play most. The SE spectrum plots every puzzle you've finished across the scale. Your arsenal counts every technique you've actually used — naked pairs, X-Wing, swordfish and further — so you can watch your repertoire grow. Every finished game replays move by move, and each history card carries a summary of the techniques that solved it.

CHALLENGE A FRIEND
Rival mode sends your exact puzzle to a friend as a share link, with your recorded run attached. They play the same grid you did and race your time move for move.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Pick Classic, Gold or Newspaper, or open the live-preview theme editor and make every cell, line and highlight yours. Built-in contrast validation keeps custom themes readable in light and dark.

DAILY CHALLENGE AND HINTS THAT TEACH
A fresh Sudoku puzzle every day, with a streak that tracks how many days in a row you solve it. Stuck? A hint walks you through the technique step by step, from naked pairs to X-Wing and beyond, so you learn why the move works and not just where it goes.
